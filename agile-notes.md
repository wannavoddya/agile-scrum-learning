Agile Methodology Notes

 1. What is Agile?

Agile is a set of principles and practices for software development
that focuses on developing flexibly, incrementally, and with
continuous customer feedback.

Agile development is adaptive rather than predictive.

2. Agile Core Values

1. Individuals and interactions
2. Working software
3. Customer collaboration
4. Responding to change

3. Principles of Agile Development

- Satisfaction through early and continuous delivery
- Welcome changing requirements, even late in development
- Deliver working software frequently
- Business people and developers work together daily
- Build projects around motivated individuals
- Face-to-face communication is effective
- Working software is the primary measure of progress
- Maintain a constant development pace (sustainable development)
- Continuous attention to technical excellence
- Simplicity – maximize the amount of work not done
- Self-organizing teams produce better designs
- Regular reflection and improvement through retrospectives
