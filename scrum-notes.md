 Scrum Notes

1. What is Scrum?

Scrum is an Agile framework used for developing products through
iterative and incremental work.

 2. Scrum Team
Product Owner

The Product Owner:

- Manages and prioritizes the Product Backlog
- Clarifies requirements
- Represents the customer
- Accepts or rejects completed work

 Scrum Master

The Scrum Master:

- Removes obstacles
- Facilitates Scrum events
- Coaches the team
- Protects the team from interruptions

 Developers

Developers are responsible for creating the product. They can include:

- Programmers
- UI/UX Designers
- Testers
- Database Developers
- DevOps Engineers
- Security Engineers

 3. Scrum Events

 Sprint

A Sprint is a fixed time-box, usually 1–4 weeks, in which selected
work is completed.

Sprint Planning

The team decides:

- What can be delivered
- How the work will be done
- The Sprint Goal

Daily Scrum

A short daily meeting for Developers to inspect progress and adjust
the plan.

Sprint Review

The completed Increment is demonstrated to stakeholders.

 Sprint Retrospective

The team discusses and agrees on process improvements for the next Sprint.

 4. Scrum Artifacts

 Product Backlog

A prioritized list of everything needed in the product, including:

- Features
- Bug fixes
- Technical improvements
- Security improvements
- Research tasks

 Sprint Backlog

The work selected for the current Sprint.

 Increment

The completed and usable part of the product produced during a Sprint.
