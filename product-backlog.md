Sprint Planning

Sprint 1 – Browse Products

Priority: High

Goal: Enable customers to find and view available grocery products.

User Story:
As a customer, I want to browse available grocery products, so that I can choose the products I need.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Customers can view available products.
- Products display their name, price, and availability.
- Customers can search for a product.
- Customers can select a product to view its details.


Sprint 2 – Place an Order

Priority: High

Goal: Enable customers to add products to a cart and complete an order.

User Story:
As a customer, I want to add products to my cart and place an order, so that I can purchase the products I need.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Customers can add products to the cart.
- Customers can update product quantities.
- Customers can remove products from the cart.
- Customers can confirm and submit the order.



Sprint 3 – Track an Order

Priority: Medium

Goal: Enable customers to monitor their order and delivery status.

User Story:
As a customer, I want to track my order status, so that I can know when my order will arrive.

Acceptance Criteria:

- Customers can view their order status.
- The system displays the current delivery status.
- Customers can view their order details.
