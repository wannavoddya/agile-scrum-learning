 User Stories
 Business Scenario

An online grocery ordering system allows customers to browse products,
place orders, and track their deliveries.

 User Story 1 – Browse Products
As a customer,I want to browse available grocery products,so that i can choose the product i need.

Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can view available products.
- Products display their name, price, and availability.
- Customers can search for a product.
- Customers can select a product to view its details.

Priority: High  
Business Value: Helps customers find products easily.

---

User Story 2 – Place an Order

As a customer,  I want to add products to my cart and place an order,  so that I can purchase the products I need.

Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can add products to the cart.
- Customers can update product quantities.
- Customers can remove products from the cart.
- Customers can confirm and submit the order.

Priority: High  
Business Value: Enables the core purchasing process.



 User Story 3 – Track an Order

As a customer,  I want to track my order status,  so that I can know when my order will arrive.

 Acceptance Criteria

- Customers can view their order status.
- The system displays the current delivery status.
- Customers can view their order details.

Priority: Medium  
Business Value: Improves customer visibility and satisfaction.



 User Story Format

A user story follows this format:

> As a [user], I want to [action], so that I can [benefit].

 INVEST Principles

Good user stories should be:

- Independent
- Negotiable
- Valuable
- Estimable
- Small
-Testable
